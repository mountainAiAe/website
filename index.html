Questions

Do we need a call first?

No. Send your website on WhatsApp or email. The free demo is built only from information already public on your site, and you test it on your phone.

Will the assistant make up prices?

No. It answers only from the prices, policies and FAQs you approve. Anything uncertain is handed to your team.

What does the pilot cost?

AED 1,500 fixed for 14 days, credited in full against the full setup (AED 7,500 + AED 1,250/month) if you continue within 30 days. No lock-in.

What happens to our customers' data?

The free demo uses only your public website. In a pilot, the assistant collects only what is needed to hand the enquiry to your team (clinics: booking details only, no medical records). We process it on your behalf under a written agreement signed before go-live that names every provider and the storage location, and logs are deleted or returned 30 days after the service ends. Privacy notice

What results are guaranteed?

None. We don't promise a number of bookings. The end-of-pilot report shows what actually happened: enquiries handled, out-of-hours enquiries, qualified leads and handovers.
