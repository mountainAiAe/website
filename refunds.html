Refund policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Services

Service work is scoped and quoted before it begins, and we do not take payment until the scope is agreed in writing. If we have not started work, you can cancel for a full refund. Once work has started, you pay for what has been done, and anything paid beyond that is returned.

If a delivered service does not match the written scope, tell us within 14 days. We will correct it or refund the part that was not delivered.

Digital files

Downloadable files are refundable within 14 days if the file is faulty, does not match its description, or cannot be opened in the format listed. Because a downloaded file cannot be returned, we do not offer refunds for change of mind once the file has been downloaded.

Books

Books published under the Yalla Finds imprint are sold through Amazon, and Amazon's own returns and refunds policy applies to them. We cannot process a refund for a purchase made on Amazon.

How to ask

Email info@mountainai-ae.com with your order reference. You can find it on the order status page. We reply within two working days and process approved refunds to the original payment method.

Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the consumer law that applies where you live.
