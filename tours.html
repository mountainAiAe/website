Why it matters

Visitors and residents usually message several operators at once, often in the evening, and book with whoever answers clearly first. In Harvard Business Review’s study of online sales leads, firms that contacted a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as firms that waited longer (source). A complete first reply also saves the three follow-up messages it normally takes to learn the date, group size and pickup.

What the assistant does

Complete requests

Private yacht charters, shared and dhow dinner cruises, desert safaris and city tours: it collects the trip, date, start time and hours, adults and children with ages, hotel pickup or marina, and extras.

Right trip for the group

Asks for a rough budget or boat size, and for desert trips checks for pregnancy, back or heart problems and babies, offering the camp-only option.

Safe by default

Questions from tour operators

Do we need a call or a meeting for the free demo?

No. Send your website, trip list or Instagram on WhatsApp or email. The demo is built only from information you already publish, and you test it on your phone like a guest would.

Will it quote prices or confirm bookings?

No. It quotes only your current published prices or tells the guest the team will send a written quotation. It never confirms a booking, a boat or a time slot itself: your team confirms every booking in writing.

Will it take payments or ask for card details?

No. It never asks for card numbers, CVV or OTP codes in chat. Payment goes only through your official payment link or invoice, sent by your team.

What does it say when guests ask about the weather?

It states your own written weather policy (reschedule or refund) and that the captain or the authorities decide whether it is safe to sail. It never promises the weather or a guaranteed sailing.

Can it handle desert safaris as well as boats?

Yes. For desert trips it asks whether anyone in the group is pregnant, has back or heart problems or is a baby, and suggests the camp-only option for them, so your team can confirm before booking.

What about children and ID?

It asks for children’s ages so your team can check capacity, child life jackets and child prices, and reminds guests that everyone needs valid ID on board (passport, or Emirates ID for UAE residents). Set and state your own rules; it follows them.

What happens with complaints, lost items or refunds?

They go straight to a person. The assistant hands over at once for complaints, lost items, accidents, a guest unwell on a trip, or anyone asking about refunds.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It answers in English or Arabic, whichever the guest writes in, including late evenings and weekends.

Who pays the WhatsApp message fees?

Meta’s WhatsApp fees are billed to you directly by Meta or your provider, not marked up by us. Replies to a guest within 24 hours of their message are free. Our free guide explains the fees.

What does it cost after the demo?

The 14-day live pilot is AED 1,500 fixed, credited in full against the full setup (AED 7,500 + AED 1,250/month) if you continue within 30 days. No lock-in. We don’t promise a number of bookings; the pilot report shows what actually happened.
