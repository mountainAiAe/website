Questions from clinics

Do we need a call or a meeting for the free demo?

No. Send your website on WhatsApp or email. The demo is built only from information already public on your site (treatments, hours, location, insurers you list), and you test it on your phone like a patient would.

Will it give medical advice or diagnose patients?

No. It only answers the questions you approve (hours, location, parking, treatments offered, insurers accepted) and collects booking details. It never diagnoses or promises results. Urgent messages are flagged to your team straight away, and emergencies are told to call 998 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Will it confirm appointments or quote prices?

No. It never confirms a slot on its own and only mentions prices you have published or approved. Your front desk confirms every booking.

Does it handle insurance questions?

It asks whether the patient is insured or self-pay and which insurer, and answers 'do you accept my insurance?' only from your approved list. It never confirms coverage: your team checks eligibility and any pre-approval, as today.

Does it connect to our booking or clinic-management software?

In the pilot, each booking request is sent to your front desk on WhatsApp or email and your team books it in your existing system, so nothing changes for them. A direct connection is only scoped if you ask for it and your software allows it.

Where is patient data stored?

By default it collects only booking details (treatment, preferred time, new or existing patient, insurance or self-pay, name), not medical records. UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 generally requires health data to be stored inside the UAE, with limited exceptions, so the storage set-up is agreed with you in writing before any live pilot. Data is used only to run your assistant and deleted on request.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It answers in English or Arabic, whichever the patient writes in, including evenings and Fridays.

Who pays the WhatsApp message fees?

Meta's WhatsApp fees are billed to you directly by Meta or your provider, not marked up by us. Replies to a patient within 24 hours of their message are free. Our free guide explains the fees.

What does it cost after the demo?

The 14-day live pilot is AED 1,500 fixed, credited in full against the full setup (AED 7,500 + AED 1,250/month) if you continue within 30 days. No lock-in. We don't promise a number of bookings; the pilot report shows what actually happened.
