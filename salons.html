Questions from salons

Do we need a call or a meeting for the free demo?

No. Send your website, price list or Instagram on WhatsApp or email. The demo is built only from information you already publish, and you test it on your phone like a client would.

Will it confirm bookings or quote prices?

No. It only quotes prices from your published price list, and says so when a price depends on hair length or design. It never confirms a slot itself: reception confirms every booking.

Can it handle home-service bookings?

Yes. It asks whether the client wants the salon or a home visit and, for home visits, which area, so your team can check travel time and any area charge before confirming. Offer home service only if your licence covers it.

What happens with bridal and group bookings?

It collects the date, location, number of people and whether a trial is wanted, then hands the request to your manager. High-value bookings go to a person, not a template.

What about first-time colour or lash tint?

It can tell first-time colour or lash clients that a patch test may be needed before the appointment, following your own product's instructions, so the test fits before the booking.

What if a client writes about a reaction after a treatment?

It doesn't give medical advice. It flags the chat as urgent for your manager, and for swelling of the face, lips or tongue or trouble breathing it tells the client to call 998 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It answers in English or Arabic, whichever the client writes in, including late evenings and weekends.

Who pays the WhatsApp message fees?

Meta's WhatsApp fees are billed to you directly by Meta or your provider, not marked up by us. Replies to a client within 24 hours of their message are free. Our free guide explains the fees.

What does it cost after the demo?

The 14-day live pilot is AED 1,500 fixed, credited in full against the full setup (AED 7,500 + AED 1,250/month) if you continue within 30 days. No lock-in. We don't promise a number of bookings; the pilot report shows what actually happened.
