Before the pilot: what we need from you

Most of the setup is our work. From your side, four things (or fill in the free Pilot Prep Worksheet (PDF) and send us a photo):

  1. Approve the answers. We draft them from your website — services, prices (or “price on request”), opening hours, location, booking and cancellation rules, and your 10 most common questions. You correct and approve; the assistant uses nothing else.
  2. Name who gets the handovers. One staff WhatsApp number or email, plus the hours they cover. Urgent or uncertain messages go there.
  3. Choose the channel.
    • Website chat: nothing to register. Your web person adds one short snippet to your site.
    • WhatsApp: the account stays in your company's name in Meta Business (free). A staff member with admin access approves the connection on Meta's own screen — we never ask for passwords. You can use a separate number for the assistant, or, through an approved Meta provider, keep your current WhatsApp Business app number working on your phone alongside it (Meta calls this “coexistence”; it switches off disappearing messages, view-once, live location and broadcast lists on that number — Meta's details). We go through the trade-offs with you before anything is switched.
  4. Agree what success looks like. For example: out-of-hours enquiries answered, qualified requests passed to staff, response time. Those are the numbers in your end-of-pilot report.

The WhatsApp connection depends on Meta's own checks, so timing can vary; website chat can usually start first. Ask us on WhatsApp what applies to your business.

Questions

Will it make up prices? No. It only uses the prices and policies you approve and hands anything else to your team.

Do we need new software? No. It runs on your existing WhatsApp Business number (via Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, account in your company's name) or as a chat on your website.

What if we don't continue after the 14 days? Nothing more is owed. We switch the assistant off, your WhatsApp account stays in your company's name, and you keep the end-of-pilot report.

Does it answer in Arabic? Yes. The pilot runs in English and Arabic, using the same approved answers in both languages.

What happens to our data? Used only to run your assistant; deleted on request when you stop. See our privacy notice.
