This is an estimate from your inputs, not a forecast or a promise. A month is counted as 4.33 weeks. An assistant can only recover enquiries you are losing because nobody replied in time — it does not change your prices, reviews or availability. The best way to know your real numbers is to measure them for 14 days, which is what the pilot does.

How the numbers are worked out

  1. Enquiries lost per month = enquiries per week × 4.33 × % arriving when nobody can reply × % of those lost.
  2. Customers lost per month = enquiries lost × your own conversion rate.
  3. Revenue at risk = customers lost × average first-sale value. Repeat visits are left out on purpose, so the figure is conservative.
  4. Break-even = AED 1,250 (our monthly fee after setup) ÷ average first-sale value, rounded up.
