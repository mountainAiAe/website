Questions from rental companies

Do we need a call or a meeting for the free demo?

No. Send your website on WhatsApp or email. The demo is built only from information already public on your site (fleet, rates you publish, policies), and you test it on your phone like a renter would.

Will it quote rates or confirm a car?

It only uses the rates and policies you approve. It never confirms a car, a price or availability on its own: every booking is confirmed by your staff, and anything it is unsure about is handed to a person.

Does it work on our existing WhatsApp number?

Yes. It runs on your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, with the account in your company's name, or as a chat on your website. No new software for your team.

Can it handle monthly and long-term rental enquiries?

Yes. For monthly renters it asks the rental length, budget, car category, and licence and deposit questions based on your rules, so your team only calls leads that are ready to book.

Does it collect licence and passport copies?

Only if your process needs it. By default it tells renters which documents to bring at pick-up, based on your rules. Any data is used only to run your assistant and deleted on request.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It answers in English or Arabic, whichever the customer writes in, day and night, including Fridays and late-night airport arrivals.

Who pays the WhatsApp message fees?

Meta's WhatsApp fees are billed to you directly by Meta or your provider, not marked up by us. Replies to a customer within 24 hours of their message are free. Our free guide explains the fees.

What does it cost after the demo?

The 14-day live pilot is AED 1,500 fixed, credited in full against the full setup (AED 7,500 + AED 1,250/month) if you continue within 30 days. No lock-in. We don't promise a number of bookings; the pilot report shows what actually happened.
