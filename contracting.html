Why it matters

Quote requests often arrive while estimators are on site or driving between jobs, and they go cold fast. In Harvard Business Review's study of online sales leads, firms that contacted a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as firms that waited longer, and the median response time was 42 hours (source). A customer asking three contractors for a quote usually talks first to the one who answers.

Questions from contractors

Do we need a call or a meeting for the free demo?

No. Send your website on WhatsApp or email. The demo is built only from information already public on your site (services, areas covered, how you quote), and you test it on your phone like a customer would.

Will it quote prices or per-sqm rates?

No, unless you tell it to. Most fit-out and renovation work is priced after a site visit, so by default it says so politely and passes the details to your estimator. It only ever mentions rates you have published or approved, and never invents one.

Can customers send photos, videos or a floor plan?

Yes. It asks for photos or a layout drawing if the customer has them, and they are attached to the lead summary your estimator receives, so the first call can be about the site visit.

Will it answer questions about permits and approvals?

Only with wording you approve, for example which approvals you normally handle. It never promises that a permit will be granted or how long an authority will take; your team confirms what each project needs. Our free Quote-Request Checklist has a quick map of the Dubai authorities most often involved.

Does it book site visits by itself?

No. It collects the job type, area and emirate, approximate size, start date and name, then hands a one-screen summary to the right person on your team, who agrees the site-visit time with the customer.

Does it connect to our CRM or estimating software?

In the pilot, each lead summary is sent to your team on WhatsApp or email, so nothing changes in how they work. A direct connection is only scoped if you ask for it and your software allows it.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It answers in English or Arabic, whichever the customer writes in, including evenings, Fridays and site days.

What customer data does it keep?

Only the project details and name the customer shares to get a quote. Data is used only to run your assistant, stays under your control under a written data-processing agreement, and is deleted on request. See our privacy notice.

Who pays the WhatsApp message fees?

Meta's WhatsApp fees are billed to you directly by Meta or your provider, not marked up by us. Replies to a customer within 24 hours of their message are free. Our free guide explains the fees.

What does it cost after the demo?

The 14-day live pilot is AED 1,500 fixed, credited in full against the full setup (AED 7,500 + AED 1,250/month) if you continue within 30 days. No lock-in. We don't promise a number of projects won; the pilot report shows what actually happened.
